Our Menu

Chicken

Our chicken dishes are prepared with fresh, never-frozen poultry that is marinated overnight. The marinade is a simple blend of lemon, garlic, parsley, and a touch of olive oil which keeps the meat tender and full of flavor. We roast the chicken at a high temperature to crisp the skin while keeping the inside juicy.

For a balanced plate, we serve the chicken with seasonal vegetables that are lightly sautéed in olive oil and finished with sea salt. If you prefer a little heat, ask for our house chili glaze. It adds a gentle kick without overpowering the natural taste of the chicken.

Beef

We use high-quality beef that is trimmed carefully and seasoned simply with cracked pepper and kosher salt. The steaks are seared in a hot pan to build a deep golden crust, then finished in the oven to the temperature you request. This approach locks in the juices and brings out the natural richness of the meat.

Each beef plate comes with roasted potatoes and a small serving of our pan sauce made with caramelized onions, fresh thyme, and a splash of stock. The result is a savory, slightly sweet sauce that complements the beef without being heavy or overly salty.

Sushi

Our sushi is prepared to order using fish delivered daily from trusted suppliers. The rice is seasoned with rice vinegar and a hint of sugar to create a clean, slightly tangy base for each roll. We offer familiar options like salmon and tuna, along with a rotating selection of seasonal specials.

If you are new to sushi, we recommend starting with a simple cucumber roll or a classic California roll, then exploring nigiri pieces one by one. Let us know your preferences and we’ll guide you to options that match your taste and comfort level.
